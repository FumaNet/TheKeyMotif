\paragraph*{S4 Appendix.}
\label{S4_Appendix}
{\bf Sensitivity of the KlebPhaCol evaluation to medium, label mapping and host
scope.} The external evaluation involves three choices that the main text
reports under a single primary setting. This appendix reports every stratum
under all combinations of those choices, so that the primary results can be
judged against the alternatives.

First, KlebPhaCol reports host-range values in two growth media. LB is used
throughout the main text; TSB is reported here as a sensitivity analysis.

Second, KlebPhaCol distinguishes productive infection from undetermined lysis.
Because the source matrices underlying our training data confirmed positives by
planktonic killing rather than plaque formation, neither mapping is
unambiguously correct: the strict mapping excludes undetermined lysis, and the
permissive mapping treats it as positive. Base rates differ substantially
between the two, which is why lift rather than bare PR-AUC is the comparable
quantity.

Third, 16 of the 74 KlebPhaCol hosts carry capsule types absent from the
training vocabulary and therefore receive an all-zero serotype encoding. Metrics
are reported both across all hosts, which measures deployment on an unseen
capsule vocabulary, and restricted to hosts whose capsule type the model was
trained to represent, which measures the model as designed.

Confidence intervals are given two ways. Pair-level intervals resample pairs and
treat each as independent. Phage-level intervals resample KlebPhaCol phages, and
are the appropriate interval wherever test proteins are concentrated on a small
number of training matches: in the near-identical stratum all 34 RBPs match one
of two training phages, so pair-level intervals there understate the uncertainty
considerably.
